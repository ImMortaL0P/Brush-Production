**Tips**

- **Wrong text:** if a sticker's text comes out garbled, regenerate just that sticker with "the text reads exactly: '…'".
- **Consistent style:** upload 2–3 PNGs from `stickers/png/` as style references.
- **Printing:** Canva won't add a CutContour cut line. Send the final PNG back so it can be rebuilt as a layered SVG with a proper die-cut path.

Shipping and Cancellation Policy This Shipping and Cancellation Policy explains how order processing, shipping, delivery delays, damaged shipments, and cancellations are handled for purchases made on Posterized.in. Shipping Policy: Shipping Locations: We ship across India. International shipping is available upon request. Shipping rates and delivery timelines may vary depending on the destination. Any applicable customs duties, taxes, or additional charges for international orders are the responsibility of the customer. Order Processing Time: Posterized.in operates on a print-on-demand model. All orders are printed, packed, and dispatched within 1 to 2 business days from the date of order confirmation. Processing time may vary slightly during high order volume, sale periods, or due to design/file-related issues. Shipping Methods: We work with trusted courier partners to ensure safe and timely delivery. Available shipping options and applicable charges, if any, will be displayed at checkout before order placement. Estimated Delivery Time: Metros and Tier 1 cities: 3 to 5 business days after dispatch Tier 2, Tier 3 cities and remote areas: 5 to 10 business days after dispatch Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to courier delays, public holidays, weather conditions, network disruptions, customs clearance, or other external factors beyond our control. Posterized.in is not liable for delays caused by the courier partner. Order Tracking: Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a shipping confirmation email, SMS, or WhatsApp update containing the tracking details. You can use the tracking details to monitor your order status online. Shipping Delays: While we try our best to ensure timely delivery, delays may occur due to circumstances beyond our control. In case of significant delays, customers may contact our support team for assistance, and we will coordinate with the courier partner wherever possible. Incorrect or Incomplete Shipping Address: Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete shipping details at checkout. Posterized.in is not responsible for failed deliveries, delays, returns, or losses caused by incorrect, incomplete, or undeliverable address details. Re-shipping charges will apply in such cases. Lost or Damaged Shipments: In rare cases where a shipment is lost or arrives damaged, please contact our support team immediately. We will coordinate with the courier partner and assist with a suitable resolution after verification. Important Note on Damaged Packages: If the outer packaging appears visibly damaged at the time of delivery, please refuse to accept the parcel and inform our support team immediately. If the package is accepted and the product inside is damaged, defective, missing, or incorrect, customers must provide a clear and continuous unboxing video of the sealed package within 48 hours of delivery . Claims without valid proof may not be accepted. Cancellation Policy: Custom and Personalised Orders: Orders for customised or personalised products, including custom posters, custom split sets, and personalised photo prints, are strictly non-cancellable once placed. No exceptions will be made as production begins immediately upon order confirmation. Non-Customised Orders: Cancellation requests for standard non-customised products are accepted only before the order is dispatched. Once the order has been shipped, cancellation will not be possible. How to Request Cancellation: To request cancellation for an eligible order, please contact us immediately at support@posterized.in or via WhatsApp with your order number. This Shipping and Cancellation Policy is subject to change without prior notice. Customers are responsible for reviewing this policy before placing an order. Need Help? For any questions or concerns, please contact us at support@posterized.in .

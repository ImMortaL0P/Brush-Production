Every poster on Brush is printed to order on 300 GSM matte stock and shipped from our Patna studio. Browse by category in the sidebar, or search by title — filters and search combine, so you can narrow a category down further by keyword.

Before you order

A5 is 14.8 × 21 cm, A4 is 21 × 29.7 cm, and A3 is 29.7 × 42 cm. A4 is the base size on every listing; A5 and A3 are offered as size swatches on the product itself, with the price difference shown right on the swatch.

80 GSM is our standard matte stock — light, affordable, and the default on every product. 140 GSM is a noticeably thicker, premium matte paper that holds color better and feels closer to a gallery print; it's a paid upgrade selectable on the product page.

Every poster is print-on-demand, so orders are printed, packed and dispatched within 1-2 business days. After dispatch, metros and Tier 1 cities typically take 3-5 business days, while Tier 2/3 and remote areas take 5-10 business days.

Since every piece is custom printed, we don't offer standard change-of-mind returns — but if your order arrives damaged or incorrect, we'll replace it once you share an unboxing video. See our full returns policy for details.

Shipping Policy

This policy details our procedures regarding order processing, deliveries, and delays for all purchases made at Brush.

Shipping Locations

We provide nationwide shipping across India. For international orders, please contact us directly, as shipping rates and timelines will vary. Customers are responsible for any applicable customs duties or international taxes.

Processing & Dispatch

As Brush operates on a print-on-demand basis, all orders are custom printed, securely packed, and dispatched within 1 to 2 business days after order confirmation. During high-volume periods or sales, processing may take slightly longer.

Estimated Delivery Timelines

  • Metros & Tier 1 Cities: 3 to 5 business days post-dispatch.
  • Tier 2, Tier 3 & Remote Areas: 5 to 10 business days post-dispatch.

Please note that these are estimates. We are not liable for delays caused by external factors such as courier network disruptions, weather conditions, or public holidays.

Tracking & Delays

Once your order leaves our facility, you will receive tracking details via email or SMS. If you face significant delivery delays, our support team is happy to assist in coordinating with our courier partners.

Address Accuracy

Customers must provide complete and accurate shipping details. Brush is not responsible for failed deliveries or losses due to incorrect addresses. If an order is returned to us for this reason, reshipping charges will apply.

Lost or Damaged Shipments

If your package appears visibly damaged upon delivery, please refuse to accept it and contact us immediately. For internal damage, refer to our Refund Policy regarding the mandatory 48-hour unboxing video requirement.

Cancellation & Returns

Cancellation Policy

Custom & Personalised Orders: Orders featuring custom artwork, personalised prints, or split sets cannot be cancelled once placed. Production begins immediately upon confirmation to ensure fast delivery.

Standard Orders: Cancellations for non-customised products are only permitted before the order has been dispatched. Once shipped, the order cannot be cancelled.

To request an eligible cancellation, please contact us immediately at support@brush.com with your order number.

Return Policy

We do not accept standard returns. Replacements are only offered in cases where an incorrect or damaged product is delivered. We do not provide replacements for:

  • Change of mind or accidental orders.
  • Incorrect size, quantity, or design selected by the customer.
  • Slight color variations between your screen and the final print.
  • Low-quality images uploaded by the customer for custom orders.

Refund Policy

Refunds and replacements are strictly governed by the conditions below to ensure fairness and quality control.

Mandatory Unboxing Video

To be eligible for a replacement or refund for a defective, damaged, or mismatched item, you must provide a clear, continuous unboxing video. The video must start before the sealed package is opened and clearly display the shipping label, the opening process, and the defect. This video must be emailed to support@brush.com within 48 hours of delivery. Claims without a valid unboxing video will be rejected.

Resolution Process

Once we verify your unboxing video, our primary resolution is to issue a replacement. Refunds are only processed if a replacement is impossible. Approved refunds will be credited back to your original payment method within 5 business days.

RTO (Return to Origin) & Failed Deliveries

If an order is returned to us due to customer unavailability, refusal to accept, or an incorrect address, it is treated as an RTO. For prepaid standard orders, courier and handling fees will be deducted before any refund is issued. Custom orders are strictly non-refundable even in RTO scenarios. If you request reshipping of an RTO parcel, additional shipping charges must be paid upfront.
